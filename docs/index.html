Why it matters

Over 50 lakh people in India have a hearing disability. How many people around them can say hello back?

The law is already there

The RPwD Act 2016 promises education in sign language, interpreters on TV, and teachers trained in ISL.

The people aren't, yet

Most hearing people have never learned a single sign. Learning resources are mostly videos with no feedback.

Haath Se closes the gap

Rights first, then practice with instant feedback, so a first sign takes minutes instead of a course.

Questions

Good to know

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Hand tracking and recognition run in your browser. The server only stores hand-point numbers recorded by organisers, small reference photos, and Sign Rush scores.

Is this a sign language translator?

No. Haath Se is a practice tool for hearing people learning their first ISL signs. Real conversations need fluent signers and qualified interpreters.

Where do the signs come from?

Numbers 1 to 9 and letters A to Z are built in, from an open ISL gesture chart (MIT licence) that we turned into hand-tracking data, so the camera can check them with no setup. The 30 expressions are built in too: we measured the hand movement from community ISL avatar clips and play it back as an animated figure, with no video copied. Every sign links to ISLRTC so you can check the official form.

Why can't it check moving signs?

Haath Se compares hand shapes, not motion. For signs that move, the camera checks the main hand shape, and ISLRTC shows you the movement.

Why "Deaf" and not "deaf and dumb"?

Many Deaf people use their voice, and "dumb" and "mute" are hurtful and inaccurate. The RPwD Act itself uses "deaf" and "hard of hearing".
